Kindle reading diagnostic

Local development tool. Reads device files only: no book changes, cache writes, self-test writes, or server uploads. Normal reading detection remains disabled.

  1. Disconnect Kindle in any other Kindle Bridge tab first.
  2. Before plugging it in, note examples that your Kindle shows as unread, partly read (with percentage), and read. Add their titles below.
  3. Connect and collect once. Download the report and attach it in our conversation.

The report contains exact filenames, raw sidecar bytes, checksums, and parse errors, including unsupported sidecar files. It does not download the book files. Limits and any skipped files are included.
